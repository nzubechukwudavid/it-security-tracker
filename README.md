# IT -> Security Career Track & Study Cockpit

A personalized, all-in-one study cockpit and job tracker designed to take you from a computer science/tech background into Enterprise IT Support, Network Engineering (Cisco CCNA), and Cybersecurity Operations.

[**Launch Live App**](https://it-security-tracker.vercel.app) | [**1-Click Action Hub**](https://david-job-finder.vercel.app)

---

## What is This?

Preparing for certifications and hunting for jobs at the same time usually means juggling open browser tabs, lost bookmarks, scattered flashcards, and messy spreadsheets.

This project combines everything into a single, clean workspace:

- **A Daily Study Cockpit**: Watch your CCNA video lectures, review flashcards, practice IP subnetting, and look up Cisco commands all in one screen.
- **A 16-Week Step-by-Step Roadmap**: A structured checklist covering everything from your initial setup and hands-on networking to security labs and interview prep.
- **An Automated Job Tracker**: Connects directly to an autonomous AI job bot that finds new entry-level roles every 4 hours, tracking what you have applied to, interview invitations, and fresh openings.
- **Seamless Phone & Desktop Sync**: Study at your desk on your PC, then scan a QR code to continue reviewing flashcards on your phone with zero setup.

---

## Main Features

### 1. The Study Cockpit (Daily Learning Hub)
- **Built-in Video Player**: Streams high-definition downloaded CCNA lessons locally for instant seeking with zero buffering. If you are away from your PC, it automatically plays the official YouTube lesson instead.
- **Picture-in-Picture Mode**: Pop the video out into a floating window that stays on top while you practice in Cisco Packet Tracer or a terminal.
- **2,100+ Practice Flashcards**: Digital Anki flashcards built right into the app using spaced repetition to help you remember key networking concepts.
- **Interactive Subnetting Gym**: Practice calculating IPv4 network addresses, broadcast IDs, and usable hosts under speed with instant answers.
- **1-Click Cisco Command Cheatsheet**: Copy day-by-day Cisco IOS configuration commands straight to your clipboard with one click.
- **Study Time Tracker**: Tracks your active study hours and predicts your target certification readiness date without stressful countdown timers.

### 2. The 16-Week Working Track (Roadmap)
- **98 Structured Checkpoints**: Organized into 4 clear phases:
  - **Phase 0 (Weeks 0-1)**: Pre-study logistics, hardware setup, and strategy.
  - **Phase 1 (Weeks 2-10)**: Cisco CCNA 200-301 complete curriculum.
  - **Phase 2 (Weeks 11-13)**: Hands-on Security Operations (SOC), SIEM logs, and incident handling.
  - **Phase 3 (Weeks 14-16)**: Resume refinement, technical interviews, and job offer negotiations.
- **Independent Task Ticking**: Mark off items as you finish them. Checking off days in the study cockpit automatically updates your roadmap progress.

### 3. Automated Job Search Integration
- **Live Connection to AI Job-Finder**: Reads data from the autonomous job scraper running every 4 hours via GitHub Actions.
- **Application Status at a Glance**: Automatically tracks applications sent, active interview calls, unapplied fresh roles, and roles you passed on.
- **1-Click Action Hub**: Includes a direct button to open the live job board whenever you want to apply to newly discovered roles.

### 4. Cross-Device Cloud Sync
- **Local-First Speed**: Loads instantly offline from your browser storage.
- **Phone Pairing**: Tap **More -> Cloud Sync** to view a QR code or copy a 1-tap link. Open it on your iPhone or Android to study on the go with real-time sync.

## How to Run It

### In Your Browser (Easiest)
Open [**https://it-security-tracker.vercel.app**](https://it-security-tracker.vercel.app) on any phone, tablet, or PC. Everything works immediately out of the box.

### On Desktop with High-Speed Local Video
If you have downloaded CCNA videos on your computer:
1. Double-click the **Study Cockpit** shortcut on your Windows Desktop (or run launch_cockpit.bat).
2. It silently starts the local media streamer in the background and opens the cockpit in your browser.
3. You get instant, zero-latency 1080p scrubbing through all lecture videos.

### On Your Mobile Phone
1. Open the cockpit on your PC.
2. Click **... More** at the top right and open **Cloud Sync & Mobile Pairing**.
3. Scan the QR code with your phone camera to pair both devices automatically.
